# 小狐狸 look mechanics

Keep the natural fox torso, all paws, hips, and bushy tail anchored to the same ground point and practical size. Turn the head and upper neck with believable fox anatomy. Let the ears, cheek fur, and ruff follow slightly after a head turn and settle; keep individual fur markings, cream muzzle/chest/tail tip, leg coloration, and eye construction fixed to the anatomy. Move both eyeballs in their sockets as complete natural eye surfaces with stable iris, pupil, eyelids, and catchlights; do not slide pupils independently. The muzzle and black nose point with the skull. Allow a small shoulder shift only when a larger turn needs it. Do not rotate or warp the entire fox, stretch the body, or alter natural proportions.

Use viewer coordinates: 000 looks toward the top edge, 090 toward screen-right, 180 toward the bottom edge, and 270 toward screen-left. Make each cardinal unmistakable through nose direction, visible eye placement, muzzle overlap, ear overlap, and head/ruff silhouette at pet size. Intermediate steps should smoothly connect those anchors. Keep paws on a shared baseline, size close to the idle pose, realistic red-orange guard hairs and cream underfur, natural soft lighting, and a transparent background without scenery, accessories, labels, effects, shadows, or detached fragments.
